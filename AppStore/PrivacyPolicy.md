# Privacy Policy — beeWatch

_Last updated: 2026-09-22_

> **Published at:** https://giovannicoppola.github.io/beeWatch/privacy.html
> (source: `docs/privacy.html` — edit both together)

beeWatch ("the app") is an independent, third-party client for Beeminder. This
policy explains what the app does with your information. It is written to be
accurate for the app as shipped; if the app changes, this policy will be updated.

## The short version
- beeWatch has **no servers of its own** and **no analytics or advertising**.
- The only place your data goes is **Beeminder** (beeminder.com), at your
  direction, to show your goals and log your datapoints.
- Your Beeminder **API key** is stored **on your device** and shared only between
  your own iPhone and Apple Watch.

## What the app stores
- **Beeminder API key.** Stored locally on your device (and in a shared app group
  so the watch app and its complications can use it). It is synced between your
  own iPhone and Apple Watch using Apple's WatchConnectivity. It is never sent to
  the developer or any third party other than Beeminder itself (as the
  authentication token on your Beeminder API requests).
- **App preferences** (e.g. reminder settings, your most-frequent entry values).
  Stored locally on your device.

## What the app sends, and to whom
- **Beeminder API (https://www.beeminder.com).** The app makes requests to
  Beeminder to fetch your goals and datapoints and to submit new datapoints. These
  requests include your API key and travel over HTTPS. Your use of Beeminder is
  governed by Beeminder's own privacy policy and terms.

## What the developer collects
- **Nothing.** The developer operates no backend and receives no personal data,
  usage data, or diagnostics from the app.

## Data retention & deletion
- Because data is stored only on your device, you can remove it at any time by
  deleting the app, or by clearing the API key in the app's Settings. Data held
  in your Beeminder account is managed through Beeminder.

## Children
- The app is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect information from children.
  It collects no personal information at all.

## Changes to this policy
